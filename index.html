Talk faith with your son.

A five-minute conversation, built in.

Boys learn what a man is by watching one. Use each week's man to start the talks most fathers never know how to begin. Ten minutes at the kitchen table, in the truck, or at bedtime is enough.

  1. Tell the story in your own words.
  2. Ask what stands out to him.
  3. Ask where he sees himself in the man.
  4. Share one thing from your own life.
  5. Pray a one-sentence prayer.
